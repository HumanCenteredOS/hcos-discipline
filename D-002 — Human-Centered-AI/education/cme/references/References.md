# References

## The Future of AI May Feel Like One Relationship

**Version:** 1.1  
**Last reviewed:** July 28, 2026

## Reference Scope

These sources provide background on trustworthy AI, health-specific ethics and governance, algorithm transparency, lifecycle risk management, and patient safety.

They do not establish the HCOS™ terminology used in this activity. HCOS™ and the Identity, Authority, and Delegation Stewardship concepts are original conceptual work and are identified separately below.

## HCOS™ Conceptual Attribution

Jones C. **The Future of AI May Feel Like One Relationship: An HCOS™ Insight and Architectural Forecast.** Sophia Care Alliance / Human-Centered Operating Systems™; 2026.

Related original HCOS™ concepts used in this activity include:

- Identity, Authority, and Delegation Stewardship;
- Contextual Identity;
- AI Continuity Layer;
- Delegated Authority Method;
- Personal AI Memory Governance;
- Human Load Protection;
- and the principle that continuity should not collapse identity, context, memory, authority, capability, or responsibility.

These terms represent original conceptual work by Carey Jones, PharmD. They are not presented as established scientific consensus, regulation, or an accreditation standard.
