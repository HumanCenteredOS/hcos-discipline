## Versioning Convention

- **Major version:** Substantial change to the activity’s purpose, learning objectives, central framework, or assessment strategy.
- **Minor version:** Meaningful revision to slide sequence, scenarios, narration, knowledge checks, or references.
- **Patch version:** Typographical, formatting, accessibility, or administrative correction that does not change educational meaning.
