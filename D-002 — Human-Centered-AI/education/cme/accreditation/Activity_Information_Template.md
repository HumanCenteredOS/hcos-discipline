## Learning Objectives

After participating in this activity, learners should be better able to:

1. Differentiate a continuous AI experience from the distributed systems operating underneath it.
2. Describe why healthcare AI requires context-bound identity, delegated authority, and governed memory.
3. Identify boundary risks when AI moves across patient, clinician, organizational, payer, caregiver, and family contexts.
4. Apply a practical human-centered stewardship lens before AI-supported work moves from information to action.
