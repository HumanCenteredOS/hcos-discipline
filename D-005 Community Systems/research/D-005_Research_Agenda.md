# D-005 Community Systems Research Agenda

## Priority Questions

- How should cumulative community risk be measured?
- How can lived experience be included without losing evidence discipline?
- Which burden-transfer patterns recur across sectors?
- How should rural, urban, tribal, and regional contexts differ?
- Which protections fail most often in practice?
- How should historical inequity be represented?
- How can communities monitor recovery without overburdening residents?
- Which interventions produce benefits across multiple systems?
