# Community Systems — ELI5

A community is made of many connected parts.

Schools, hospitals, roads, farms, jobs, housing, internet, insurance, government, and families all affect one another.

Sometimes one group fixes its own problem by pushing the work onto someone else.

HCOS helps the community see:

- what is connected,
- where pressure is moving,
- who is carrying it,
- what protection is missing,
- who can make a change,
- and whether the community can recover.

The goal is not to blame one group.

The goal is to help everyone see the same system and make better decisions together.
