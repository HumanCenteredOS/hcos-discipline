# D-005 Community Systems — Discipline Architecture

## 1. Applied Discipline Function

D-005 extends HCOS from organization-scale analysis to community-scale systems.

A community includes many institutions with different missions, authorities, budgets, data, and accountability structures.

Residents experience their combined effects.

## 2. Scope

D-005 includes:

- community systems mapping,
- cross-sector interdependencies,
- pressure and burden transfer,
- access and protection gaps,
- cumulative community risk,
- public participation,
- decision rights and accountability,
- community resilience,
- disaster recovery,
- institutional trust,
- public claims and lived-experience signals,
- rural, urban, tribal, regional, and place-based systems.

## 4. Relationship to Other Disciplines

- D-001 provides universal human-systems principles.
- D-002 informs AI-enabled community systems and public-sector technology.
- D-003 informs community healthcare access and health-system interactions.
- D-004 informs burden, recovery, cognitive load, and human protection.

## 5. Boundary Rule

A concept belongs in D-005 when its primary meaning depends on community-scale interaction among multiple institutions, public systems, residents, and place-based conditions.
