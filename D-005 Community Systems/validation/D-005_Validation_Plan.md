# D-005 Validation Plan

## Validation Stages

1. Expert review
2. Resident and frontline review
3. Retrospective case testing
4. Focused community pilot
5. Inter-rater review
6. Revision and version control

## Validation Questions

- Does the discipline reveal interactions missed by sector-specific analysis?
- Are affected residents meaningfully represented?
- Can users distinguish evidence from interpretation?
- Are burden transfer and protection gaps visible?
- Do outputs support clearer decisions?
- Does the process avoid unnecessary burden?
