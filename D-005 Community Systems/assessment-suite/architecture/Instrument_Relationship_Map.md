# Instrument Relationship Map

| Instrument | Receives | Produces | Informs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Community Systems Interdependency Map | Community scope, institutions, services, population needs | System dependency map | Pressure, risk, governance |
| Cross-System Pressure Transfer Assessment | System decisions and operational changes | Pressure-transfer pathways | Human load, protections |
| Community Human Load Assessment | Resident experiences and practical burden | Human load profile | Protection gaps, priorities |
| Community Protection Gap Analysis | Pressures and existing safeguards | Protection gap map | Risk and roadmap |
| Cumulative Community Risk Map | Multiple pressures and vulnerabilities | Risk interaction map | Resilience, escalation |
| Community Decision and Accountability Map | Authorities, roles, evidence, consequences | Governance map | Action ownership |
| Community Resilience and Recovery Assessment | Preparedness, continuity, support, learning | Resilience profile | Roadmap and stewardship |
| Public Claim Evidence and Meaning Review | Public statement or concern | Claim decomposition | Scope and research questions |

## Core Flow

```text
Systems
  ↓
Pressure
  ↓
Human Load
  ↓
Protection
  ↓
Risk
  ↓
Authority
  ↓
Resilience
  ↓
Learning
```
