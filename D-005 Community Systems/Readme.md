# D-005 — Community Systems

**Discipline ID:** D-005  
**Discipline Name:** Community Systems  
**Framework:** Human-Centered Operating Systems™ (HCOS™)  
**Status:** Emerging Applied Discipline  
**Primary Steward:** Sophia Care Alliance™

## Purpose

D-005 Community Systems applies HCOS human-systems principles to communities where residents experience many institutions as one interconnected life.

It examines how healthcare, education, transportation, housing, environment, agriculture, insurance, public safety, broadband, government, employers, nonprofits, and local economies interact.

D-005 asks:

> How should communities be understood, protected, governed, and strengthened when important outcomes cross institutional boundaries?

## Relationship to D-001

D-005 is built on D-001 Human Systems.

D-001 establishes the universal principles.

D-005 applies those principles at community scale.

```text
D-001 Human Systems
        ↓
Universal HCOS principles
        ↓
D-005 Community Systems
        ↓
Community methods, instruments, governance, and stewardship
```

D-005 should not duplicate general human-systems theory. It should focus on community-scale applications such as:

- cross-sector interdependency,
- burden transfer between institutions,
- community human load,
- protection gaps,
- cumulative risk,
- public participation,
- accountability across organizational boundaries,
- resilience,
- recovery,
- and public meaning.

## Core Principle

> People experience systems as one life, even when institutions govern them as separate domains.

## Initial Assessment Suite

The first applied capability within D-005 is the:

**HCOS™ Community Systems Assessment Suite**

It contains eight instruments:

1. Community Systems Interdependency Map
2. Cross-System Pressure Transfer Assessment
3. Community Human Load Assessment
4. Community Protection Gap Analysis
5. Cumulative Community Risk Map
6. Community Decision and Accountability Map
7. Community Resilience and Recovery Assessment
8. Public Claim Evidence and Meaning Review
