# HCOS™ Community Assessment Governance Guide

## Purpose

Protect legitimacy, participation, evidence integrity, privacy, and accountability during community systems assessment.

## Governance Requirements

### Scope Authority

Document who authorized the review and what decisions it may inform.

### Participation

Include people who:

- deliver services,
- receive services,
- carry informal caregiving or coordination work,
- possess relevant evidence,
- and experience disproportionate consequences.

### Evidence Integrity

Maintain provenance and distinguish evidence from interpretation.

### Privacy

Use the minimum necessary personal information.

### Conflict of Interest

Disclose financial, political, institutional, or professional interests that could affect interpretation.

### Accountability

Identify who owns follow-up actions and who reviews unintended consequences.

### Public Communication

Do not overstate certainty. Explain what is known, uncertain, disputed, and still under investigation.

## Core Governance Question

> Who has the authority to create or reduce community risk, and how are they accountable to the people carrying the consequences?
