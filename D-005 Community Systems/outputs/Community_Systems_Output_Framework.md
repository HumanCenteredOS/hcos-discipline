# Community Systems Output Framework

A complete D-005 assessment may produce:

1. Community Systems Map
2. Cross-System Pressure Map
3. Community Human Load Profile
4. Community Protection Gap Map
5. Cumulative Community Risk Map
6. Decision and Accountability Map
7. Resilience and Recovery Profile
8. Evidence-Based Community Findings
9. Prioritized Community Resilience Roadmap
10. Community Learning Report
