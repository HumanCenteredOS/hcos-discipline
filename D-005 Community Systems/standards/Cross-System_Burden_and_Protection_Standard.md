# HCOS™ Cross-System Burden and Protection Standard

## Standard Statement

Community institutions should evaluate whether policies, service changes, resource decisions, and performance goals transfer unreasonable work, cost, delay, risk, or responsibility to other systems or populations.

## Required Questions

- What problem is the decision trying to solve?
- Which burden is reduced?
- Which burden may be transferred?
- Who will carry the transferred burden?
- Do they have adequate resources and authority?
- Which protections exist?
- Are protections accessible in practice?
- Which groups may be disproportionately affected?
- How will cumulative effects be monitored?
- Who is accountable for unintended consequences?
