# D-001 Validation Framework

## Validation Goals

Determine whether D-001 principles:

- apply across industries,
- remain useful over time,
- improve decision quality,
- reduce unsupported individual blame,
- reveal hidden system conditions,
- and support human flourishing.

## Evidence Sources

- healthcare,
- education,
- public sector,
- community systems,
- technology,
- workforce,
- nonprofit organizations,
- and cross-cultural applications.
