# Human Systems — ELI5

A human system is any group of people trying to do something together.

It might be:

- a family,
- a school,
- a hospital,
- a business,
- a government,
- or a community.

People do not work alone. They are affected by rules, tools, information, leaders, time, money, and the way work is organized.

HCOS looks at all of those things together.

The goal is not to blame people.

The goal is to understand what the system is helping people do, what it is making harder, and what should change so people can do meaningful work safely and well.
