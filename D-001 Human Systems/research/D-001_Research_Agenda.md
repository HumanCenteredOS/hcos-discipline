# D-001 Human Systems Research Agenda

## Priority Questions

- How should human-system health be assessed without oversimplification?
- How does pressure move through complex systems?
- What conditions preserve wisdom, compassion, presence, and meaning?
- How should lived experience be incorporated as evidence?
- How should responsibility and authority be evaluated?
- How do systems learn without destabilizing essential operations?
- Which protections remain universal across industries and cultures?
- How should cumulative burden and recovery capacity be measured?
