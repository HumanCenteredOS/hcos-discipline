# HCOS™ Human Systems Assessment Method

## Purpose

Provide a universal method for examining a human system before recommending change.

## Assessment Sequence

1. Define the purpose of the system.
2. Identify the people who depend on it.
3. Establish the system boundary.
4. Gather documentary and experiential evidence.
5. Map workflow and interdependencies.
6. Examine the eight system forces.
7. Identify human pressure and protection.
8. Clarify authority, responsibility, and escalation.
9. Separate observation from interpretation.
10. Prioritize changes.
11. Evaluate unintended consequences.
12. Document learning and stewardship needs.

## Required Evidence Categories

- documented process,
- actual practice,
- stakeholder experience,
- performance measures,
- policies,
- technology,
- authority structure,
- resources,
- outcomes,
- uncertainty.

## Core Output

A trustworthy explanation of:

- what is happening,
- why it may be happening,
- who is affected,
- what the system is asking people to carry,
- which protections are present or absent,
- and what should be examined next.
