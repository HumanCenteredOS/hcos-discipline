# HCOS Discipline Relationship Map

## D-001 and D-005

| Discipline | Role | Primary Question |
|---|---|---|
| D-001 Human Systems | Foundational discipline | How do human systems function and flourish? |
| D-005 Community Systems | Applied discipline | How should communities be understood, protected, and improved? |

## Architectural Relationship

```text
HCOS Foundations
      ↓
D-001 Human Systems
      ↓
Universal principles, methods, and standards
      ↓
D-005 Community Systems
      ↓
Community-specific methods, governance, instruments, and outputs
```

## Placement Rule

Place a concept in D-001 when it remains true across organizations, communities, industries, and technologies.

Place a concept in D-005 when its meaning depends on community-scale interaction among multiple institutions, residents, public systems, and place-based conditions.
