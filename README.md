# D-001 — Human Systems

**Discipline ID:** D-001  
**Discipline Name:** Human Systems  
**Framework:** Human-Centered Operating Systems™ (HCOS™)  
**Status:** Active Architecture  
**Primary Steward:** Sophia Care Alliance™

## Purpose

D-001 Human Systems is the foundational HCOS discipline for understanding how people, relationships, roles, workflows, policies, technologies, resources, decisions, incentives, and environments interact as a system.

It establishes the universal principles that apply across organizations, communities, healthcare, education, government, nonprofits, families, and AI-enabled environments.

D-001 does not focus on one industry or setting.

It asks:

> How do human systems function, what makes them healthy, what creates unnecessary suffering, and what conditions help people flourish?

## Discipline Role

D-001 provides the conceptual foundation for applied HCOS disciplines.

Examples include:

- D-002 Human-Centered AI
- D-003 Healthcare Systems
- D-004 Human Load Protection
- D-005 Community Systems

Applied disciplines may introduce context-specific methods and instruments, but they should remain grounded in the universal human-systems principles established here.

## Core Questions

- What is a human system?
- Why do human systems exist?
- How do system forces shape human experience?
- How do authority, knowledge, workflow, policy, technology, and resources interact?
- How is pressure created, transferred, absorbed, or reduced?
- What protections help people remain wise, compassionate, present, and connected to meaning?
- How do healthy systems learn, adapt, and steward the future?
